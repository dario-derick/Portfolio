About

A developer and teacher on a continuous learning journey.

I combine a background in education with a growing career in software development, building tools that serve real people and communities.

My journey into technology started with curiosity about how digital tools can make everyday work easier. That curiosity has grown into a commitment to learning software development, from crafting responsive interfaces with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to exploring Python and generative AI for building practical applications.

Before and alongside my tech journey, I trained as a teacher and earned a Diploma in Teacher Education. That background shapes how I approach every project: I explain things clearly, I design with empathy, and I care deeply about whether something is genuinely useful to the person using it.

I am particularly interested in how technology can support education, small businesses, and social impact by creating tools that are accessible, affordable, and genuinely helpful. I am open to internships, entry level roles, freelance work, and collaborative projects where I can contribute and grow.
